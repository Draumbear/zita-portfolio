Designer Inspired

Rewind

Project 1 Bachelor Fashion Technology — Hogent 2022–2023

For our first project we had to develop a brand to design a commercial collection of sleeveless tops and bustiers for spring/summer 2024.

Our team chose the theme 'rewind', where we took inspiration from historical corsets and bustiers, mainly from the renaissance. We were influenced by the luxurious fabrics, tight bustiers and voluminous sleeves.

After further research, we decided not to limit ourselves to only the renaissance period, but to take inspiration from different eras and put our own twist to it.

Our brand is called Ederra, which is Basque for 'beautiful'. Corsets and corset tops arose from wanting to obtain a beauty ideal in which the waist, bust and hips were greatly emphasized. This beauty ideal was anything but comfortable. With our brand Ederra, we want to give beauty a new perspective by designing bustiers that are comfortable but still aesthetic so every woman feels beautiful and powerful.

Our vision

As a team, we looked for a good coherence between tranquility, romance and elegance. We want our audience to understand that clothing is more than just a piece of fabric around the body, it is something to make them feel good. Therefore, our clothing is also of high quality so that our customers feel comfortable but also stylish.

With our corsets, we want our audience to be able to express their personality and emotions. Our customer should feel confident, elegant, beautiful and at peace with themselves.

The colour palette

Original colour palette

Originally our team decided to work with soft pastel colours because we thought they complimented our vibe more. Once we started to actually develop our collection into tangible pieces of clothing, we changed our mind.

Actual colour palette

With our inspiration mainly coming from the renaissance, we looked back on the colour palette of that time period. We ended up choosing a deep blue, olive green and cream white to soften it out.

My design

Process

Each team member in our group had to design five different tops. Out of those five, our jury chose one design we had to actually create. This was the top they chose for me.

My design has a base bustier with an added bust piece in the front. The bust piece and sleeves are both frilled, which makes for a lot of volume that gives it a dramatic touch. In the back the top closes with hooks, which are hidden.

This was my first prototype. Made out of a thick cotton fabric for a good base. Here I tried to give the sleeves and bust part volume with pleats, but the result wasn't succesful. The base bustier was a bit too big, especially around the bust.

For the second prototype I used frills instead of pleats, which gave much more volume to the sleeves and bust part. The base bustier fit perfectly after making some changes.

This was the final fitting of the top in the actual fabric. Here I just had to finish the hem and put in the closing. The actual fit of the top was great.

Our collection

Personal reflections

I was quite nervous at the start of this project. Designing a commercial collection involves a lot. From finding brand names and sources of inspiration to looking at competition, designing and finally executing those ideas. I was scared of the great responsibility such a project brought, and didn't really know what to expect from my group.

Besides, I am also a big perfectionist, which definitely has its positive side because I am always very punctual and finish everything to perfection. The disadvantages of this are that I impose the same high standards I set for myself on other people, which can cause tension. Despite my perfectionism, chaos does reign at times, something other team members also experienced.

Within our group, I therefore had the role of bringing order. This included going over schedules, dividing tasks, looking at deadlines ... This gave a better overview of the task and a clear path to the finish line for both me and my team members.

I thoroughly enjoyed seeing the evolution my bustier made from technical drawing to finished design. In particular, I found the pattern drawing the most interesting, as that was the biggest challenge in making my design perfect. I am proud of what I was able to make and am also immensely grateful to my team for the smooth running of this project.
