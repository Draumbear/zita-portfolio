All Season

Smart Design

Project 2 Bachelor Fashion Technology — Hogent 2023–2024

Our second project is themed Smart Design. For this project we were asked to develop an innovative and sustainable product that complies with the circular economy. Through a questionnaire answered by more than 600 people our All Season team designed a collection of 9 modular coats that can be worn every season.

The concept

The main goal of our project was to battle overconsumption. We thought about the fact that a lot of people have many different jackets and coats hanging in their closets. Ones for spring, summer, fall and winter. For warmer and colder temperatures, for rain and sun. The All Season coat you can wear every season because of our modular parts.

Our coat exists out of three parts: you have the base jacket, an extension piece which makes it a coat and a sleeveless bodywarmer that can be worn under the coat. The hood is removable, when you are not using it, it can be changed out for a small strap. For the closing of the coat and the attachment of our modular parts we use clothing magnets. This is a very user-friendly method for a wide audience.

Our designs

Our brand identity

Our All Season brand stands for simplicity, quality and sustainability. We wanted to design coats that are minimalistic but classy. We have a very basic color palette so our jacket can be worn with many different outfits. Our modular design allows our customer to customize the jacket to their own style and needs.

Our Sample

For the presentation of our collection we worked out one of our designs. We used a sand-coloured softshell fabric for the coat itself. The design is asymmetrical, the closing has a diagonal shape and there is a wavy decorative stitching on it. This wave is a signature symbol of ours. On the left side of the base jacket there is a big pocket, as well as on the right side of the extension piece. These pockets are quite big so you can fit a lot of stuff in them. The hood is removable and can be changed out for a small strap. The bodywarmer is made of a fleece fabric in our signature blue colour. The collar is high so it covers your throat and rises a little above the collar of the jacket. Our wavy decorative stitching covers the surface of the fabric.

The materials

For our coat we use three different Polartec Fabrics: the Polartec Power Shield for the outside fabric, Power Dry for the lining and Thermal Pro for the bodywarmer. The Polartec Power shield was especially designed for frequent wear and washing. The fabric is made out of 48% non food biological Non-GMO materials. Polartec uses Biolon for this, a plant based nylon material that is renewable and sustainable. The Power Shield is a woven softshell fabric. It exists out of three layers: a waterproof outside layer, PFAS-free membrane and an inside layer. Molecular diffusion takes place between these different layers, drawing water vapor away from the body while the outer fabric provides a barrier against water droplets. On the outside of the fabric there is a PFAS-free DWR-coating, which makes the fabric extra waterproof.

The Polartec Power Dry fabric is a bi-component technology made out of recycled PET plastic. Because of the different yarn structures and different densities in the fabric moisture constantly evaporates, there's thermoregulation which keeps the fabric dry and the body warm.

Polartec Thermal Pro is a fabric made entirely of 100% recycled post consumer PET waste. It is a synthetic fleece fabric that provides warmth to the wearer without additional weight, making it easy to carry as well. The fabric uses high-pile fibers, which creates air pockets that provide thermoregulation combined with breathability. The body is insulated, preventing heat from escaping. Thanks to the high-pile fibers, the knitted fabric also has no pilling effect. Because of that fiber breakage is prevented and the number of released fiber particles is reduced by 85%.

We use a couple of other materials in our coat. We sew our coats with the Resortecs Smart Stitch 190° yarn. It's a heat soluble yarn made of plant based polyamide. The yarn dissolves at 190°C to nothing, which makes it easy to recycle our fabrics after they are used.

For the closing of the bodywarmer we use a YKK zipper. Lastly we also have two labels in the collar of the coat: one with our logo and one with the size. These are also made with 100% post consumer PET plastic.

How we close the loop

Production

For the production of our coats we have our own atelier. They are entirely made in Belgium. Our coats are made on demand, which means that we only make them once we get the order. For the start of our production part of the team will sew the coats. When we grow to be a bigger brand we will hire seamstresses to help us out. With this strategy we don't have an excess stock in our atelier.

Sale

We sell our coats in the concept store Icon, located in Brussels. Here our customers try them on and order them. Once they are ordered they get made in our atelier. After a maximum waiting time of 2 weeks the coat gets delivered at the customers' house or the Icon store. In the store there is only one size of every coat available. This makes that we don't have an excess of fitting samples. These samples get sold at a lower price once we launch a new collection.

Use

When our consumer wears our coat, we advise not to wash it too much. You can always hang the coat outside to air out and store it inside out of the sunlight when you're not wearing it. In the jacket there is a printed QR code which the buyer can scan to get more information on the jacket. In this digital booklet you can find the washing instructions and some more information on the coat and our brand.

End of life

If a modular part of the coat breaks down, they can always order that specific part again. This so they don't have to buy a whole new jacket.

After the buyer makes good use of our coat and it's worn down, they can bring it back to the Icon store or ship it back to us. We will recycle the fabrics so the fibres can be used again for a new product. As we only have 3 different fibres this will be pretty easy to do, thanks to the help of the recycling company Simpany.
