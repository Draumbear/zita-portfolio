About

Hello, I'm Zita.

I am a recent Fashion Technology graduate from University College Ghent, where I developed a strong background in technical garment construction, bespoke pattern making, and gained three years of industrial sewing experience.

Before that, I studied Textile Design at the KASK School of Arts for two years, where I gained a lot of knowledge on weaving, knitting, screenprinting and print design.

I am a hard worker, highly organised, meticulously detail-oriented and perform well in a team setting. I tackle projects with a positive, hands-on approach. I'm excited to bring my production skills and strong work ethic to a dynamic, creative team.

In the future, I am hoping to gain more knowledge in finishing techniques, bespoke tailoring, draping and haute couture, and would love to eventually follow an Haute Couture Embroidery course.

In my free time I love to be creative, hike, travel, go to art workshops and participate in cultural exchanges around the globe. I also like to make my own clothes.
